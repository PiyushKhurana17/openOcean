# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

OpenOcean is committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that participation in our community and project will be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, political belief, profession, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Helping others learn and grow
- Giving credit and acknowledging contributions

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Insulting or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
- Trolling, flaming, or deliberate provocation
- Unwelcome sexual attention or physical contact
- Threats or violent language

## Enforcement

Community members who violate this code of conduct will be approached and asked to cease immediately. Repeated violations may result in:

1. Temporary mute (24-72 hours)
2. Temporary suspension from discussions
3. Permanent ban from the project

The severity of the violation and pattern of behavior will be considered.

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness a violation of this code of conduct:

1. **Document**: Take note of specific incidents, timestamps, and individuals involved
2. **Report**: Email a detailed report to [maintainer email] with subject line: "Code of Conduct Violation"
3. **Safe Response**: All reports are treated confidentially
4. **No Retaliation**: We do not tolerate retaliation against reporters

Your report should include:
- Description of the incident
- Individuals involved (if known)
- Witnesses (if any)
- Timeline of events
- Any supporting evidence (screenshots, logs, etc.)
